# Mathematical Axioms of Governance Engineering (SBCM v2.0 - v4.0)

This document defines the core mathematical framework of the **Governance Engineering** protocol. It provides the unified equations required to model and debug resource circulation from regional to interstellar scales.

## I. Fundamental Units & Mass Definitions
The base metrics for quantizing social and economic influence.

### 1. Statistical Normalization (Quantum of Governance)
Normalizes heterogeneous municipal data into a standardized governance unit ($B_{std}$).For the Japan 2024 Model, it is defined as:
  
$$B_{std} = \frac{P_{total}}{N_{muni}} \approx 72,176 \text{ [persons/block]}$$

(Note: Rounded to $7.2 \times 10^4$ for general engineering specifications.)

### 2. Complex Economic Mass (Vectorial Wealth)
Distinguishes tangible resources from information-based credit. The Phase Angle ($\theta$) measures systemic fragility.
  
$$Z_i = M_{w,i} + iM_{c,i}$$
  
$$\theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{M_{c,i}}{M_{w,i}}\right)$$

Equations governing the movement (flux) of wealth and information.

$$\text{Payment}(t) \propto \text{Physical Progress}(t)$$

## III. Geometric Constraints & Metrics
The physical boundary conditions of the planetary and stellar vessel.

### 6. Spherical Tessellation (Zero-Sum Surface)
Defines the Earth as a closed system where expansion equals compression of adjacent domains. 
  
$$\sum V_i = 4\pi r^2 = \text{Constant}$$

### 7. Anisotropic Metric (Effective Economic Distance)
Calculates distance based on local economic conductivity ($\sigma$).
  
$$d_{eff} = \min_{\gamma} \int_A^B \frac{1}{\sigma(x)} ds$$

### 8. Spacetime Metric (Relativistic Cost Function)
Integrates gravity wells ($\Phi$) and proper time ($\tau$) into the economic cost of transport.
  
$$ds^2 = g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu = -\left(1 + \frac{2\Phi}{c^2}\right)c^2dt^2 + \left(1 - \frac{2\Phi}{c^2}\right)dx^2$$

## IV. Structural Limits & Stability
Thresholds for systemic failure, conflict, and equilibrium.

### 9. Entropic Elasticity (Mechanics of Rejection)
The restoring force that expels capital when it exceeds local potential capacity ($C_{pot}$).
  
$$\mathbf{F}_{eject} = -k(S_{current} - C_{pot})$$

### 10. Structural Buckling (The War Threshold)
The condition under which accumulated strain energy density ($u$) leads to physical rupture.
  
$$u = \frac{1}{2}\sigma^{\mu\nu}\epsilon_{\mu\nu} > \gamma_{crit} \implies \text{Structural Buckling (War)}$$

### 11. Autonomy Scaling (Multipolar Equilibrium)
The critical coefficient to prevent geometric heat death (monopoly).
  
$$\alpha = \frac{\ln(B_{std})}{\ln(M_{max})} \cdot \Phi(k) \approx 0.12$$

## V. Universal Limits & Ejection
The ultimate thermodynamic boundaries of civilization.

### 12. Thermodynamic Limit of Growth (The Thermal Wall)
Proves that complexity costs eventually exhaust all production gains.
  
$$\dot{K} = sA^\sigma K^\alpha - \delta_0 A^\gamma K \quad (\text{Limit: } \gamma \ge \sigma \implies \text{Heat Death})$$

### 13. Stellar Energy Standard (Value Grounding)
Grounds total economic value in the absolute star output ($L_\star$).
  
$$Z_{total} = \oint_{\partial V} \mathbf{S} \cdot d\mathbf{A} \le L_\star$$

### 14. Conservation of Pain (Newton’s 3rd Law)
Axiom that government revenue is a physical subtraction from private wealth.  

$$\Delta G_{revenue} = -\Delta P_{wealth}$$

### 15. Systemic Ejection (Space Colonization)
Redefines space expansion as a thermodynamic safety valve for a maturing civilization.  

$$\text{Flux}_{vert} \propto \sigma - \gamma_{sat}$$
