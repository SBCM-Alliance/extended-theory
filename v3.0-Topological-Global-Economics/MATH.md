# SBCM v3.0: Mathematical Framework of Topological Global Economics

This document defines the formal mathematical specification of **SBCM v3.0 (Standard Block Comparison Method)**. This version expands the regional resource circulation theory (v2.0) into a planetary-scale topological model.

## 1. Core Axiom: The Physicality of Information
Rooted in **Landauer’s Principle**, all economic transactions and governance processes are treated as physical work within a closed system.
- **System Constraint:** The Earth’s surface is a closed **Riemannian Manifold ($S^2$)**.
- **Conservation Law:** Total surface area $A = 4\pi r^2$ is constant.

## 2. Definition of Variables

### 2.1 Complex Economic Mass ($Z_i$)
The economic influence of a nation or block $i$ is defined as a complex vector to separate tangible work from digital signals:
$$Z_i = M_{w,i} + iM_{c,i}$$
- **Real Part ($M_{w,i}$):** Kinetic/Dynamical Mass (grounded in verified entropy reduction: labor, energy, resources).
- **Imaginary Part ($iM_{c,i}$):** Potential/Signal Mass (credit creation, speculative potential, digital fiction).
- **Geometric Weight (Attractor Strength):** $|Z_i| = \sqrt{M_{w,i}^2 + M_{c,i}^2}$

### 2.2 Anisotropic Economic Metric
The Earth's surface is not a Euclidean plane but an **Anisotropic Riemannian Manifold**. The effective economic distance $d_{eff}$ between two coordinates $A, B$ is calculated as:
$$d_{eff}(A, B) = \min_{\gamma} \int_{A}^{B} \frac{1}{\sigma(x)} ds$$
- **$\sigma(x)$ (Economic Conductivity):**
  - **Land Domain:** $\sigma_{land} \approx 1.0$ (High resistance due to borders/infrastructure).
  - **Ocean Domain:** $\sigma_{ocean} \gg 1.0$ (**Superconducting Area** with zero physical resistance).

## 3. Governing Equations

### 3.1 Planetary Multipolar Equilibrium
To prevent "Geometric Heat Death" (total domination by a single mass), we implement **Autonomy Scaling** using the **Autonomy Coefficient ($\alpha$)**:
$$\alpha = \frac{\ln(B_{std\_mass})}{\ln(M_{max})} \cdot \Phi(k) \approx 0.12$$
- **$B_{std\_mass}$:** Minimum autonomous unit (Standard Block).
- **$M_{max}$:** Maximum system mass.
- **$\Phi(k)$:** Global impedance matching factor based on institutional rigidity $k$.

### 3.2 Spherical Tessellation (Voronoi Partitioning)
The planetary surface is partitioned into $N$ polygons $V_i$ using an **Additively Weighted Geodesic Voronoi Tessellation**:
$$V_i = \{ x \in S^2 \mid d_{eff}(x, x_i) - W(Z_i) \leq d_{eff}(x, x_j) - W(Z_j), \forall j \neq i \}$$
where $W(Z_i)$ is the weight derived from the complex mass norm.

### 3.3 Structural Buckling (Conflict Threshold)
War is defined as a **First-Order Phase Transition** occurring when the interface pressure exceeds the structural limits of international law/interdependence. Derived from the **Virial Theorem**:
$$E_{strain} > E_{bond} \implies \text{Structural Buckling (War)}$$
- **$E_{strain}$ (Strain Energy):** Accumulated potential energy at the polygon interface due to misalignment of mass distribution.
- **$E_{bond}$ (Bond Energy):** The ultimate strength of diplomatic/legal boundaries.

## 4. Engineering Solutions: G-Cart Global

### 4.1 Speculation Loss (Joule Heat)
Speculation is the result of an **Impedance Mismatch** between high-frequency digital signals ($iM_c$) and low-frequency physical work ($M_w$):
$$Q_{spec} = \int I(t)^2 R_{exit}(t) dt$$
where $R_{exit} \to \infty$ at the narrow interface where digital energy fails to find a physical container.

### 4.2 The Gravity Firewall
To protect local autonomy from remote predatory capital, the cost function for procurement is hard-coded with the **Inverse-Square Law of Distance**:
$$Cost_{total} = P_{bid} + \alpha \cdot (d_{eff})^2$$

### 4.3 P-Bill (Physical Bill of Exchange)
Grounding digital potential into physical work via verifiable telemetry:
$$P\text{-}Bill = \text{Minted iff } (D_{mass}, D_{energy}, D_{space}) \text{ is verified.}$$
